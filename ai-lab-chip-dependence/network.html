Method

Each node's output survives in proportion to its inputs. Within one input type (for example photoresist), suppliers substitute in proportion to their shares; across input types a node needs all of them (a fab needs wafers and resist and lithography), so its output is limited by the scarcest type. This is the AND-dependency that the article notes plain max-flow misses. A lab's chips substitute for each other by their share of its compute. Loss = 1 − surviving output. Shares that don't add to 100% leave the remainder with unmodelled suppliers, who keep working. A failed chip, fab or packager loses 100% of its output; equipment makers and material or gas sources lose the share set by the two sliders, because tools keep running for months without their vendor and fabs hold weeks to months of materials and gas stock (the 2022 neon shock cost fabs very little output).

Worst case is a lab's largest loss over every single failure. Worst outside TSMC leaves out TSMC fabs, CoWoS and the CoWoS steps done by Amkor/ASE, which every US-aligned lab shares.

The article's flow-load benchmarks, for comparison: TSMC 71.9%, NVIDIA 62.5%, ASML EUV 61.5%, TSMC CoWoS 61.5%, JSR 31.2%, SK hynix 28.1%, TOK 7.3%. Its targeted attack removed TSMC CoWoS first (max-flow to 54%) then SK hynix (26%). Those are max-flow loads; the numbers on this page are output losses, a different measure.

Assumptions, stated so they can be challenged

  • No short-run substitution between suppliers beyond their current shares: HBM, CoWoS and leading-edge capacity are sold out through 2027, and qualifying a new resist or wafer supplier takes months.
  • Lab compute mixes are disclosed only for Anthropic (S-1: ~4.7 GW Nvidia, ~4.5 GW TPU, ~1.2 GW Trainium, ~0.8 GW AMD) and single-vendor labs (xAI, Mistral). The rest are estimates.
  • The equipment and material sliders are judgement calls. Set them to 100% to see the instant-cut-off case, or near 0% for the stockpile-and-wait case.
  • Upstream shares (wafers, resist, gases, raw sources) are global market shares applied to every Western fab, and scaled down for Chinese fabs to leave room for domestic suppliers not modelled.
  • Huawei's HiBL memory, Kunlun and ByteDance's custom chip have no disclosed upstream suppliers, so they are tested only where links are known.
