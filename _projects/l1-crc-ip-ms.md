## What it does

Analyzes immunoprecipitation–mass spectrometry (IP-MS) data to map LINE-1 protein interactions in
patient tumors, comparing **tumor**, **matched normal** tissue and **IgG controls**.

## Samples

Ten groups from three tumor sites:

| Site | Diagnosis | Conditions |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Colon | Adenocarcinoma | Tumor, normal, IgG |
| Liver | Metastatic rectal adenocarcinoma | Tumor, normal, IgG |
| Ovary | Krukenberg carcinoma | Normal, IgG (two versions each) |

## Data

Raw data are public on ProteomeXchange under identifier **PXD013743**.

Part of my [protein interaction work at Rockefeller]({{ '/projects/protein-interaction-random-walks/' | relative_url }}).
