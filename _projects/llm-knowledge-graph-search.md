## The problem

Researchers looking for drug targets need to query huge, connected biomedical knowledge — millions of
gene–disease associations — without writing graph queries themselves.

## What I built

- **An LLM-powered scientific search system** on top of biomedical knowledge graphs, letting researchers
  ask questions in natural language. It **sped up drug-target identification by 30%**.
- **A GraphRAG workflow spanning 50+ disease areas**, grounding model answers in the graph.
- **MCP tools linking three research platforms**, so agentic workflows could pull from each.
- **An evaluation framework for AI-assisted biomedical research** — to show where models quietly fail,
  not just where they succeed.
